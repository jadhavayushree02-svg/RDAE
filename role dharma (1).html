☯️SANATAN Framework — Eternal Alignment Principles
🔱 Satya (Truth)

Building systems that reflect truth — honest metrics, transparent processes, authentic role descriptions.

☮️ Ahimsa (Non-harm)

Role assignments that do not harm employee well-being, dignity, or psychological safety.

🌀 Dharma (Duty)

The natural duty each person carries — aligning role responsibilities with this inner calling.

⚡ Karma (Action)

Nishkama Karma — acting without attachment to personal reward, performing duties for universal good.
